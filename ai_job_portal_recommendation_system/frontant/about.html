About the platform

The bridge between talent and the right opportunity, built by AI.

Setu (सेतु, meaning "bridge") is an AI-powered job portal that reads real skill and real intent — not just keywords — to connect candidates and companies who actually belong together.

Job search shouldn't feel like shouting into a form.

Most portals ask people to compress years of skill into keywords and hope the filters are kind. Setu flips that — our AI reads context, growth trajectory, and real project work, then does the matching quietly in the background so both sides show up already aligned.

Process

How the AI actually matches you

Three passes, each one narrowing toward a genuinely relevant connection — not just a keyword hit.

01

Understand

Resume, portfolio links, and stated preferences are parsed into a structured skill-and-intent profile — including things candidates rarely think to type, like adjacent skills and career trajectory.

02

Rank

Every open role is scored against that profile on fit, growth potential, and culture signals from the employer — not just matching job-title strings.

03

Connect

Only matches above a relevance threshold are surfaced to either side, with a plain-language explanation of why the match was made.

What's inside
